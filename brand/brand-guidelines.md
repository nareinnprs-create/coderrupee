# Coder Rupee — Brand Guidelines (Draft 1.0)

## Brand
- Name: Coder Rupee
- Tagline: BUILD WITHOUT BOUNDARIES.
- Positioning: Premium engineering with competitive pricing.
- Personality: Intelligent, precise, resourceful, independent.

## Logo
- Use the supplied SVG files as the current working concept.
- Prefer the horizontal lockup for headers and documentation.
- Use the emblem alone for app icons, favicons, CLI identity and compact surfaces.
- Use the stacked lockup for square promotional layouts.
- Keep clear space around the complete lockup of at least half the emblem height.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, redraw, or place on visually noisy backgrounds.
- Avoid using the full wordmark below 80 px wide; use the emblem instead.

## Colors
- Carbon Forest: #141713
- Signal Lime: #D7FF64
- Studio Ivory: #F5F5EE
- Execution Mint: #7DD6C1
- Dark Surface: #1C201A
- Raised Surface: #252A22
- Primary Text: #F5F5EE
- Secondary Text: #ADB3A5
- Border: #3A4035
- Success: #22C55E
- Warning: #F2BD61
- Error: #FF766F

Use Carbon Forest and Studio Ivory as foundations. Signal Lime is the primary accent; Execution Mint is secondary. Do not rely on color alone for status communication.

## Typography
- Brand wordmark: custom geometric sans-serif direction; current SVG uses a system fallback and should be converted to outlined paths for final master delivery.
- UI: Inter or equivalent.
- Code/terminal: JetBrains Mono or equivalent.
- Confirm font licensing before redistribution or embedding.

## Cross-platform behavior
- Desktop and web: support light and dark themes.
- CLI: use a monochrome-safe emblem and text labels for states.
- Mobile: use the emblem only for launcher icons.
- Motion: restrained; respect reduced-motion settings.

## Important status
This is a first-pass vector reconstruction of the concept, not a legally cleared or final trademark master. The emblem geometry and negative spaces require designer review, optical refinement, small-size tests, and similarity screening before commercial launch. The SVG wordmark text is live text and can render differently if Inter is unavailable; convert text to paths for final distribution.
